# Unity-6-simulation
A 3D environmnet built with Unity 6, featuring directional sunlight and street lighting using point lights and spotlights, with automatic streelight actviation at night

KNUST Campus 3D Virtual Tour

An interactive 3D model of part of the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) campus, built in Unity with the Universal Render Pipeline (URP). Fly around the campus, watch a live day/night cycle, see the street lights switch on at night, and click buildings to focus the camera on them.

Scene file: Pesentation.unity

Features
Free-fly camera: smooth movement with mouse look (look angle clamped to about -60° / +63°).
Day/night cycle: the directional light (sun) rotates continuously, changing the lighting across the scene.
Automatic street lights: about 20 curved street lights, each with a point light and a spot light. They switch on when the sun drops below the horizon and off again at day.
KNUST logo fountain: stone basin, rippling water surface, centre pedestal, a central water jet plus six surrounding jets (particle systems), and a rotating KNUST emblem on top (spins at 90°/s).
Building selection: click a campus building to select it, show its name label, and smoothly move the camera to focus on it.
Campus environment: roads with crossroad markings, grass and pavements, trees, planters, trash cans, and parking bays with parked cars.
Building interiors/props: the Administration block includes chairs, drawers, shelves, couches, carpets, a bookshelf, houseplants, and ceiling lights.
Physics demo: a bouncing ball (Rigidbody + sphere collider) with a jump force script.
